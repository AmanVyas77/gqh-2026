# Exchange-limit check (post-evaluation; 4 October 2026)

CME Rule 10102.E points to the Chapter 5 position-limit table; Rule 559 governs aggregation and exemptions. Accountability thresholds and hard limits are different: exceeding an accountability level permits exchange information requests or position-reduction instructions, whereas a speculative position limit is a ceiling absent an exemption.

A stronger corroborating source than the submission's federal spot comparison exists: [CFTC final rule, footnote 594](https://www.cftc.gov/LawRegulation/FederalRegister/finalrules/2020-25332.html) describes LE's exchange single-month limit as 6,300 contracts, without an all-months-combined limit. [CME September 2025 notice](https://www.cmegroup.com/content/dam/cmegroup/notices/market-regulation/2025/09/msn09-17-25.pdf) uses a 6,300 single-month futures-equivalent limit for weekly options aggregated into Live Cattle code 48. These corroborate 6,300 as a recent benchmark; they do not prove every current row or historical amendment.

The submission's 600-contract federal spot-month benchmark does not represent a binding deferred-contract limit for the A sale-selection rule. Report 6,300 as a qualified recent benchmark alongside the documented largest LE position, not as certified current trading approval. At large AUM, order liquidity binds well before this benchmark; other positions, options and affiliated accounts must be aggregated.
